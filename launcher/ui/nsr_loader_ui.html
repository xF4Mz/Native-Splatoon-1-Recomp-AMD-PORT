Welcome to NSR

NSR runs your own copy of Splatoon (Wii U, v2.7.2) as a native PC program. Nothing to compile, nothing downloaded. Your dump is only read, never changed.

Pick your game, pick a folder, press Install. Then Play.

For developers only: this dev build (dev build) is for testing the port, not for playing through. AMD Radeon graphics only (NVIDIA and Intel untested). Expect crashes: if it breaks, open Logs and send the newest file.

Game code recompiled with DolRecomp by ExpansionPak (lead developer Hyperway), GPL-3.0. Thank you! AI-assisted development.
